All Are Welcome

Grace Abounds Here

Following the path of Christ, we celebrate diversity and believe that God's grace belongs to everyone, without exception. Regardless of your background, race, economic status, family structure, or sexual orientation, you are welcome to find a loving spiritual home at our shared table.

Celebrating Our Legacies

In 2022, three historic congregations merged to build a stronger Lutheran presence on Long Island. Today, we steward all three properties for unique ministries.

Huntington Station, NY

Our hub for consolidated Sunday Morning Worship (10:00 AM) and Sunday School (9:30 AM). On-site host to the independently operated St. Peter's Nursery Preschool, as well as choirs and community Senior Luncheons.
