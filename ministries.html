Our Outreach & Parish Ministries

Active, volunteer-run ministries that feed the hungry, build relationships, and share God's grace locally and globally.

Joseph's Storehouse Food Pantry & Closet

The primary mercy and outreach center of our parish, serving the Huntington Station community.

Located at our Gloria Dei Campus, Joseph's Storehouse is an active client-choice food pantry affiliated with the Long Island Cares and Island Harvest food banks. Rather than receiving pre-packed bags, clients select their own fresh meats, fish, local produce, refrigerated goods, and baked items to combat regional food insecurity.

Demographics & Growth

In 2025, the Pantry served **26,825 individuals** (a 6% increase over 2024), registering 291 new clients. Currently, over 1,300 households or individuals are registered. The client base includes 46% adults, 39% children, and 15% seniors. We serve over 30 known homeless clients, acquiring ready-to-eat foods to distribute.

The Clothing Closet Boutique

In addition to food, clients can "shop" in our clothing and household items Closet (opened January 2023). Under the coordination of Kathy Readinger, the Closet offers men's, women's, and children's clothing, shoes, and small household decorations, all acquired through generous local donations. Clients select 3-4 items during their weekly visit.

Food Acquisition & Alliances

Using the church-provided van, volunteers conduct **8 scheduled food pick-ups per week** from partners including Trader Joe's, Value Drugs, Whole Foods Market, Food Rescue USA, Redefine retail store, and local Girl Scout cookie drives.

Feed the Hungry Program

32 continuous years of delivering fresh protein to local families in need.

Started in 1993, the Feed the Hungry Program is a grass-roots, entirely volunteer-run mission. The program operates **52 weeks a year** without interruption, purchasing and delivering healthy, fresh proteins (meats, fish, and dairy) twice weekly to complement the non-perishable groceries distributed by the **Helping Hand Rescue Mission** on Broadway in Huntington Station.

Our Shoppers & Volunteers

Ten dedicated individuals currently take turns shopping for and delivering food items weekly: Mike Barker, Nancy Barker, Ted Gramse, Pauline Eagen, Barbara Harris, Linda Huber, Karen Lasdin, Jane Maresco, Karen Maresco, and Paul Schmelter. We are always looking for additional shoppers!

Funding Challenges

The Feed the Hungry Program does not draw from the church operating budget. Instead, it relies on local grant requests and targeted donations. We partner with local organizations like Thrivent Northeast for special food collection drives to sustain operations.

"Soup for the Soul" Ministry

"Love does not care why you are hungry. Love just pours the soup."

Launched in July 2024, "Soup for the Soul" was the very first collaborative ministry combining the volunteer forces of Gloria Dei, King of Kings, and St. Peter's. The ministry cooks and freezes nutritious, homemade soups to supplement community feeding programs.

Freezer Distribution (No Questions Asked)

Once a month, cooking volunteers gather in the kitchen to prepare the "Soup of the Month" (such as Navy Bean with Bacon or Split Pea). The soup is packaged, labeled with ingredients and a short prayer, and stored in the church freezer at the St. Peter's campus.

Parishioners are invited to take containers from the freezer to distribute to sick neighbors, homebound members, seniors in assisted living, or anyone facing a difficult week. There are no restrictions, fees, or questions asked.

Homeless Shelter Outreach (HIHI)

The soup ministry also partners with the Huntington Interfaith Homeless Initiative (HIHI), providing a full monthly meal (soup, rolls, and fixings) for HIHI clients during the winter months of shelter operations.

Guatemala Mission Team

Bringing clean water, aid, and hope to communities in Guatemala.

Our parish partners with Christ Lutheran Church in East Northport to sponsor an annual summer mission team to Guatemala. The team works directly in local communities to build and install high-quality water filtration kits.

Water Filter Builds

In preparation for the trip, the team hosts fellowship "filter build" events (partnering with the international non-profit Wine to Water). Volunteers assemble filtration kits and learn about the specific community in Guatemala we will be serving.

Fundraising & Tag Sales

To fund the filters and travel expenses, the team hosts community Tag Sale fundraisers and "Rock N Recovery" dances, accepting donations at the Gloria Dei campus stage.

Hoop Dreams Basketball Program

Serving over 600 kids in the Huntington community through sportsmanship and skills.

Led by coach Tim Carr, Hoop Dreams Basketball makes active, daily use of the gymnasium facilities at the St. Peter's Campus. The program provides a positive, structured environment for local youth, creating lifelong memories through the love of basketball.

Program Offerings

  • Competitive teams for youth of all ages.
  • Seasonal clinics and intensive summer camps.
  • K-2 Beginners Program: Introducing younger kids to basketball fundamentals, focus, and teamwork.
