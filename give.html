Stewardship Vision

Why We Give

Stewardship is more than paying bills; it is a spiritual practice. As Lutherans, we believe that everything we have is a gift from God. In giving back, we express our gratitude and join in God's mission of restoration, justice, and mercy in the world.

Your financial contributions are pooled to support our unified operating budget. This enables us to maintain all three campuses, pay our staff, fund children's Sunday School, support local recovery groups, and grow ministries like:

  • Joseph's Storehouse Food Pantry: Feeding over 26,000 community members in Huntington yearly.
  • Feed the Hungry & Soup Ministries: Providing hot soups and fresh proteins to local homeless shelters.
  • Guatemala Water Mission: Purchasing filtration kits and funding international service trips.
