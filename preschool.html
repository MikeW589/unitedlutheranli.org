St. Peter's Nursery Preschool

Hosted On-Site at St. Peter's Campus • Managed Independently

Welcome to St. Peter's Nursery Preschool

St. Peter's Nursery Preschool is a premier, licensed early childhood education center hosted on-site at the St. Peter's Campus of the United Lutheran Church of Long Island in Huntington Station.

Please note: The Nursery Preschool is an independent, non-sectarian entity. It is administratively separate from the church, maintaining its own board, staff, budgets, registration policies, and curriculum. As such, the school maintains and controls its own dedicated website.

Visit the Official Website

For parent handbooks, class schedules, tuition fees, registration forms, summer camp updates, and direct contact with the school administrators, please visit their independent website:

Go to stpetersnurserypreschool.org

Programs Offered

The preschool offers a variety of licensed programs designed to support children's emotional, social, and academic growth:

Toddlers (2s)

Focusing on early socialization, sharing, and sensory development in a nurturing space.

Nursery (3s)

Language skills, coordination, and cooperative play to build early confidence.

Pre-K (4s)

Comprehensive school readiness, phonics, math concepts, and science exploration.

Collaborations with the Church

While the preschool is administratively independent, we cherish our neighborly relationship. Once a month, the preschool students gather in the church sanctuary for a special children's chapel service. During this time, Pastor Christian sings songs and reads a bible story from "The Beginner's Bible for Little Ones". Additionally, our church music coordinator, Laurie Haddock, provides music enrichment sessions for the children.
