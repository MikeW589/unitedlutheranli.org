One Church. Three Campuses.

We are one congregation utilizing all three of our historic locations to serve the community in distinct, powerful ways throughout the week.

Worship, Education & Sunday School Hub

St. Peter's Campus

Functional Role & Midweek Ministries

St. Peter's Campus serves as our primary sanctuary for Sunday morning consolidated worship (10:00 AM) and Sunday School (9:30 AM) during the academic school year (September – June). It features an expansive sanctuary, a landmark pipe organ, and classrooms for Christian education.

Core Midweek Programs:

  • St. Peter's Nursery Preschool: An independently operated, non-sectarian early childhood partner hosted on-site, offering licensed Toddler, Nursery, and Pre-K programs.
  • Feed the Hungry Program: A 32-year volunteer-run mission delivering fresh protein weekly to local food shelters.
  • Music Ministry: Rehearsal center for our Chancel Choir, Bell Choir, and Youth Choirs.
  • Community Programs: Hosts monthly Senior Luncheons (ages 55+), Zoom Yoga with Deacon Doreen, and Hoop Dreams youth basketball camps (600+ kids).

Gloria Dei Campus

Functional Role & Midweek Ministries

Gloria Dei is the outreach and compassion heart of our collaborative parish. During the summer months (July – August), the consolidated Sunday morning worship rotates to this campus to enjoy the cozy, air-conditioned sanctuary. During the rest of the year, the campus serves as a hive of community care.

Core Midweek Programs:

  • Joseph's Storehouse Food Pantry: Serving over 26,000 individuals shopping on a client-choice model.
  • Joseph's Storehouse Closet: Coordinated by Kathy Readinger, providing clothing and home goods.
  • Recovery Groups: Hosting weekly Narcotics Anonymous (NA) recovery meetings.
  • Youth Groups: Sponsoring Boy Scout Troop 34 and Cub Scout Pack 406.
  • Study & Prayer: In-person Thursday Bible study at 7:00 PM in the Pastor's Office.

Melville Campus (King of Kings)

Functional Role & Midweek Ministries

Located on New York Avenue in Melville, NY, the King of Kings campus is structured as an ecumenical and civic partner. Sunday morning worship is rotated here for specific Holy Week liturgies, Lent midweeks, and rotating Thanksgiving Eve services. Throughout the week, the building serves as a resource for community nesting partners and civic groups.

Core Midweek Programs:

  • Nesting Congregations: Proud physical sanctuary home for the Sung Mood Korean Methodist Church and Mission Vida Church.
  • Recovery Support: Hosts weekly Melville Alcoholics Anonymous (AA) and Al-Anon group meetings.
  • Civic Anchor: Serves as a vital polling place for the Suffolk County Board of Elections during primary and general elections.
