Weekly Worship Bulletin

Sunday, June 7, 2026
  • Gathering
  • Word
  • Meal
  • Sending
  • Announcements
Welcome to United Lutheran Church! We celebrate God's grace with you today. Holy Communion is celebrated at all services and is open to all who desire to meet Christ in this bread and wine. Please follow the instructions of the pastor and ushers during the distribution.

The Gathering

PRELUDE
“Meditation” — Gabriel DuPont
WELCOME & ANNOUNCEMENTS
Leader: Please rise as you are able for the Gathering.
CALL TO WORSHIP
Leader: God calls ordinary people into lives of faith and service.
People: We trust in God's promises.
Leader: Christ welcomed tax collectors, sinners, and all who sought his mercy.
People: Christ welcomes us as well.
Leader: God brings hope where the future seems uncertain and life where all seems lost.
People: We place our trust in the God who gives life.
Leader: Let us worship the God of mercy and promise.
People: Thanks be to God.
CONFESSION AND FORGIVENESS
Leader: Blessed be the holy Trinity, ☩ one God, whose promises never fail, whose mercy reaches beyond every boundary, and whose grace gives life to the world.
People: Amen.
Leader: Let us confess our sin in the presence of God and of one another. Merciful God,
People: we confess that we do not always trust your promises. We rely on our own strength, judge others too quickly, and sometimes withhold the compassion you freely give. We struggle to believe that you can bring healing to what is broken and hope to situations that seem beyond repair. Forgive us. Renew our faith, soften our hearts, and help us follow Christ more faithfully in our daily lives.
Leader: Hear the good news. God did not wait for us to be worthy before offering grace. In Jesus Christ, sinners are welcomed, the broken are restored, and new life is given freely. Your sins are forgiven, and you are made new through God's abundant mercy.
People: Amen.
GATHERING HYMN
Will You Come and Follow Me (ELW Hymn 798)
KYRIE (ELW page 184 - read responsively)
PRAYER OF THE DAY
Leader: Let us pray.
God of promise and mercy, you called Matthew to follow you, healed those who came to you in faith, and fulfilled your promises through Jesus Christ. Strengthen our trust in your grace, especially when we cannot see the way ahead. Help us welcome others as you have welcomed us and live as people shaped by your mercy; through Jesus Christ, our Savior and Lord.
People: Amen.

The Word

Leader: You may be seated.
FIRST READING: Romans 4:13-25
A reading from Romans, the 4th chapter.
The promise that he would inherit the world did not come to Abraham or to his descendants through the law but through the righteousness of faith. For if it is the adherents of the law who are to be the heirs, faith is null and the promise is void. For the law brings wrath, but where there is no law, neither is there transgression...
Reader: The word of the Lord.
People: Thanks be to God.
Leader: Please rise as you are able.
GOSPEL ACCLAMATION
O mighty Rock, O Source of life, let your good Word in doubt and strife be strongly in us burning, that we be faithful unto death and live in love and holy faith, from you true wisdom learning. Lord, your mercy on us shower; by your power Christ confessing, we will cherish all your blessing. [ELW Hymn 786 Verse 3]
HOLY GOSPEL: Matthew 9:9-13, 18-26
The Holy Gospel according to Matthew, the 9th chapter. People: Glory to you, O Lord.
As Jesus was walking along, he saw a man called Matthew sitting at the tax-collection station, and he said to him, “Follow me.” And he got up and followed him. And as he sat at dinner in the house, many tax collectors and sinners came and were sitting with Jesus and his disciples...
Leader: The gospel of the Lord.
People: Praise to you, O Christ.
Leader: You may be seated.
SERMON
Leader: Please rise as you are able.
HYMN OF THE DAY
Jesus Calls Us; o’er the Tumult (ELW Hymn 696)
APOSTLES’ CREED
I believe in God, the Father almighty, creator of heaven and earth.
I believe in Jesus Christ, God’s only Son, our Lord, who was conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the virgin Mary, suffered under Pontius Pilate, was crucified, died, and was buried; he descended to the dead. On the third day he rose again; he ascended into heaven, he is seated at the right hand of the Father, and he will come to judge the living and the dead.
I believe in the Holy Spirit, the holy catholic church, the communion of saints, the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and the life everlasting. Amen.
PRAYERS OF THE CHURCH
Leader: Let us pray for the church, the world, and all in need... [Individual prayers read]
Leader: God of grace,
People: hear our prayer.
SHARING OF CHRIST’S PEACE
Leader: The peace of the Lord be with you always!
People: And also with you!
Leader: You may share a sign of peace with your neighbors, and then be seated.

The Meal

OFFERING & ANTHEM
“God Adoring” — Katherine K. Davis (Chancel Choir)
Leader: Please rise as you are able.
OFFERING HYMN
Praise God, from whom all blessings flow; Praise him, all creatures here below; Praise him above, ye heav’nly host; Praise Father, Son and Holy Ghost. [ELW Hymn # 884]
OFFERTORY PRAYER
Leader: Let us pray. Faithful God, receive these gifts and use them for the work of your kingdom. Through them, bring hope to those who are discouraged, healing to those who suffer, and mercy to all who are in need. We offer them in the name of Jesus Christ.
People: Amen.
THE GREAT THANKSGIVING
Leader: The Lord be with you.
People: And also with you.
Leader: Lift up your hearts.
People: We lift them to the Lord.
Leader: Let us give thanks to the Lord our God.
People: It is right to give our thanks and praise.
Leader: It is indeed right, our duty and our joy, that we should at all times and in all places give thanks and praise... we praise your name and join their unending hymn:
SANCTUS (read/sung together)
Holy, holy, holy Lord, God of pow’r and might,
heaven and earth are full of your glory.
Hosanna. Hosanna. Hosanna in the highest.
Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord. Hosanna in the highest.
THANKSGIVING AT THE TABLE & WORDS OF INSTITUTION
LORD’S PRAYER
Leader: Gathered into one by the Holy Spirit, let us pray as Jesus taught us:
Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name, thy kingdom come, thy will be done, on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, forever and ever. Amen.
INVITATION TO COMMUNION
Leader: Come to the table of Christ. Here we receive not a reward for our achievements, but the gift of God's grace. Here faith is strengthened, mercy is shared, and Christ meets us with the promise of new life.
Leader: You may be seated for communion.
COMMUNION DISTRIBUTION & MUSIC
All are welcome at Christ’s table. Wafers are to be dipped into the chalice of wine, or individual communion cups are available.
Distribution Hymns today:
All Are Welcome (ELW 641), As the Grains of Wheat (ELW 465), Lamb of God (ELW 336)
Leader: Please rise as you are able. The body and blood of our Lord Jesus Christ strengthen you and keep you in his grace.
People: Amen.
POST-COMMUNION PRAYER
Leader: Let us pray. Gracious God, we give you thanks for this meal and for the presence of Christ among us. Strengthen us through this gift so that we may trust your promises, share your mercy, and serve our neighbors with faith and compassion; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
People: Amen.

The Sending

Leader: Please rise as you are able for the blessing.
BLESSING
Leader: May the God who made a promise to Abraham strengthen your faith. May Christ Jesus fill your life with mercy and compassion. And may the Holy Spirit guide you in hope and peace, now and always.
People: Amen.
SENDING HYMN
I’m So Glad (ELW Hymn 860)
DISMISSAL
Leader: Go in peace. Serve the Lord.
People: Thanks be to God.
POSTLUDE
“March in D” — Flor Peeters
ASSISTING IN WORSHIP TODAY:
Rev. Christian Cederstrom, Pastor Nancy Barker, Worship Assistant Rich Harris, Reader and Communion Assistant Richard Whitten, Minister of Music/Organist Laurie Haddock, Minister of Music/Youth & Family
